What you are looking at

Five things an installer checks on a drawing. Each is a label on the model above, and each is here in words.

  1. Output shaft

    38 mm across and 80 mm long, with a 10 mm key. Order it smooth for a clamping hub.

  2. Mounting flange

    300 mm across, four 14.5 mm holes on a 265 mm circle. The machined spigot centres the motor on the gearbox.

  3. Terminal box

    On top, with two M25 cable glands facing right when you look at the shaft. It can be turned in 90° steps on site.

  4. Fan and cowl

    A shaft-mounted fan pushes air along the fins. Leave 40 mm clear behind the grille.

  5. Stator and rotor

    Copper windings in a laminated core, around a rotor on two sealed bearings. The air gap is 0.3 mm. Shown in the cutaway.
